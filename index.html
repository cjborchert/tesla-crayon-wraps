Photo tips
  • Lay the page flat on a surface that isn't white, like a darker table or wood.
  • Fit the whole sheet in the frame with a little table showing all around it.
  • Shooting at an angle to avoid glare is fine. Keep your shadow and reflections off the page.
  • Use the photo straight from the camera. Photos sent through chat apps are made smaller and blurrier.
  • Colouring over the lines is fine: each panel only takes colour from inside its own outline.

What usually helps
    Step 1 of 2

    Mark the corners of the page

      The outline may not line up everywhere

      In the alignment check, the pink lines should sit on the printed lines of the drawing. Where they don't, colours can end up on the wrong part of the car.

      Not lined up right?

      Use letters, numbers, spaces, dashes or underscores (up to 26 characters).

      3

      Put it on the car

      With the Tesla app

      1. Have the PNG on your phone: save it from Crayon Wraps, or AirDrop it from your computer.
      2. In the Tesla app, go to Creations → Wrap → Upload and pick the file. This needs app version 4.59 or later.

      Storage is limited this way: the app holds up to 10 wraps. Add more with a USB flash drive.

      With a USB flash drive

      1. Use a drive formatted as exFAT or FAT32 (not NTFS), with no map or software update files on it.
      2. Make a folder called Wraps at the top level and copy the PNGs into it (up to 10).
      3. Plug the drive into the car.

      Then in the car: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps. More detailed instructions are in Tesla's custom-wraps page on GitHub.
