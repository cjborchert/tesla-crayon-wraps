Photo tips
  • Lay the page flat on a surface that isn't white.
  • Fit the whole sheet in the frame with a little table around it.
  • Shooting at an angle to avoid glare is fine.
  • Colouring over the lines is fine: each panel only takes colour from inside its own outline.

    Put it on the car

    With the Tesla app

    1. Have the PNG on your phone: save it from Crayon Wraps, or AirDrop it from your computer.
    2. In the Tesla app, go to Creations → Wrap → Upload and pick the file. This needs app version 4.59 or later.

    Storage is limited this way: the app holds up to 10 wraps. Add more with a USB flash drive.

    With a USB flash drive

    1. Use a drive formatted as exFAT or FAT32 (not NTFS), with no map or software update files on it.
    2. Make a folder called Wraps at the top level and copy the PNGs into it (up to 10).
    3. Plug the drive into the car.

    Then in the car: Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps. More detailed instructions are in Tesla's custom-wraps page on GitHub.

    Runs entirely in your browser: photos never leave your device.
